Academic Support & Coaching

Matric & FSc Coaching Academy on Raiwind Road, Lahore

Proud Academy is a coaching and exam preparation academy at Main Sher Shah, 100 Feet Road, Raiwind Road, Lahore, offering Matric (Class 9 and 10), FSc and subject coaching from Monday to Saturday, 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. It provides focused academic support, concept clearing, examination preparation, and personalized guidance to help students improve their academic performance. Our academy is tailored for students seeking serious scholastic elevation.

Performance Model

Core Academic Framework

Targeted academic guidance designed to eliminate doubts, reinforce subject mastery, and maximize examination marks.

Concept Clearing

Step-by-step deconstruction of complex topics so students understand underlying concepts thoroughly rather than relying on guesswork.

Exam Preparation

Intensive preparation featuring past paper analysis, time-management techniques, question phrasing strategies, and revision schedules.

Regular Assessment

Structured periodic tests and diagnostic evaluations that pinpoint individual weaknesses early for immediate corrective focus.

Student Guidance

Individual academic advice and motivational mentorship helping students overcome academic anxiety and build steady momentum.
