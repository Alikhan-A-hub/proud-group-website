Matric Exam Preparation: 7 Tips for Class 9 and 10 Students

29 October 2026•6 min read•Proud Group of Institutions

Home / Blog / Exam Tips

Quick answer: Start early with a weekly study plan, understand concepts before memorizing, practise past papers under time limits, take regular tests, and leave the last weeks for revision, not new topics.

Board exams in Class 9 and 10 decide a lot: FSc admission, college choices and confidence for the years ahead. These seven habits make the biggest difference.

1. Make a weekly plan, not a daily panic

Divide each subject into chapters and give every week a target. A simple timetable on the wall works better than a long list in your head.

2. Understand first, then memorize

Definitions and formulas stick when you know why they are true. If a topic feels confusing, clear it with a teacher before moving on.

3. Practise past papers

Past papers show which questions repeat, how marks are divided and how much time each part needs. Try at least the last five years for every subject.

4. Write answers by hand, with a timer

Many students know the answer but run out of time. Practise full papers in the real time limit so your writing speed matches the exam.

5. Take regular tests

Weekly or monthly tests show your weak chapters early, while there is still time to fix them.

6. Keep the last month for revision

Do not start new chapters in the final weeks. Revise short notes, formulas and the questions you got wrong in tests.

7. Sleep, eat and take short breaks

A tired mind forgets quickly. Seven to eight hours of sleep and short breaks between study sessions help you remember more.
