Our Foundation

Who We Are & Our Journey

Proud School & Academy (Proud Group of Institutions) was established in 2010 with a clear, steadfast mission: to bring quality, disciplined, and concept-oriented education to the growing community along Raiwind Road in Lahore.

Over the past decade and more, we have developed into a trusted educational home comprising two dedicated campuses: Proud School at Village Bhobtian, offering solid foundational schooling, and Proud Academy at Main Sher Shah, delivering targeted coaching, assessment, and board examination preparation.

Distinct Advantage

What Makes Us Different

Unlike large impersonal institutes or isolated tuition centers, Proud School & Academy bridges foundational schooling with high-yield coaching under one coordinated philosophy:

  • Dual-Campus Synergy: Dedicated campuses for core schooling (Village Bhobtian) and academic coaching / exam prep (Main Sher Shah).
  • Established Track Record: Serving families along Raiwind Road with enduring community trust and stability.
  • Supportive & Disciplined Culture: A calm, safe, and focused atmosphere where student accountability and respectful conduct are actively maintained.
  • Individual Academic Attention: Regular assessments, actionable feedback, and targeted concept clinics for students needing extra clarity.
