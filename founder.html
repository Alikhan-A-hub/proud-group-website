A Message to Parents & Students

Dear Parents and Students,

When we opened the doors of Proud School, our aim was simple: to give the children of Raiwind Road an education that builds both the mind and the character. We believed then, as we do now, that education is more than books.

Over the years Proud School and Proud Academy have grown into a family of teachers, students and parents who share the same goal. Our teachers work to make every concept clear, our classrooms are calm and disciplined, and our doors are always open to parents who want to be part of their child's progress.

We want every student who walks through our gates to learn deeply, grow in confidence and good manners, and succeed in their studies and in life. Our motto, “Be Proud of Nation”, reminds us that the purpose of learning is to serve our families, our community and our country.

Thank you for the trust you place in us. I invite you to visit our campuses, meet our teachers and see our students at work.
