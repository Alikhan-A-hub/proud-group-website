About Proud School & Academy

A Commitment to Education in Lahore

For more than a decade, Proud School & Academy (Proud Group of Institutions) has been committed to providing quality education, academic guidance, and a supportive learning environment. We believe in nurturing both intellectual sharpness and strong moral character.

Our goal is to help students:

  • Learn core concepts deeply
  • Grow in character & confidence
  • Improve academic performance
  • Build lifelong self-discipline
  • Develop strong moral values
  • Prepare thoroughly for the future
