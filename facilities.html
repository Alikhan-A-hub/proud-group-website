Campus Infrastructure & Environment

Campus Facilities & Spaces

Disciplined, Safe & Stimulating Environments for Everyday Learning

Educational Setting

Our campuses at Village Bhobtian and Main Sher Shah on Raiwind Road are designed to provide purposeful educational environments—ensuring comfortable seating, adequate ventilation, attentive supervision, and distraction-free academic focus.

Learning SpacesAREA 01

Modern Classrooms

Well-ventilated, well-lit, and thoughtfully arranged classrooms designed to maximize focus, comfortable seating, and clear sightlines.

Key Features:

  • Ergonomic seating arrangements
  • Optimized natural lighting and ventilation
  • Interactive boards and visual learning aids
  • Discipline-focused learning layouts
Campus AtmosphereAREA 02

Supportive Learning Environment

A disciplined yet warm atmosphere where students feel encouraged to ask questions, engage actively, and develop academic curiosity.

Key Features:

  • Calm and positive academic atmosphere
  • Zero-tolerance for disruption
  • Peer collaboration zones
  • Mentorship-driven supervision
Academic LabsAREA 03

Science & Practical Learning Spaces

Dedicated spaces for experimental demonstration, helping students observe theoretical science concepts in practical, demonstrable ways.

Key Features:

  • Essential science demonstration equipment
  • Hands-on experimental learning exercises
  • Strict safety protocols and supervision
  • Concept reinforcement through demonstration
Resource CentersAREA 04

Library & Reading Corners

Quiet reading collections offering curriculum references, literary works, general knowledge books, and self-study resources.

Key Features:

  • Curated curriculum reference materials
  • Quiet study desks for independent review
  • Reading programs to foster language fluency
  • Regularly updated educational literature
Holistic GrowthAREA 05

Student Activities & Co-Curriculars

Structured co-curricular opportunities including speech, declamations, quizzes, celebrations, and character development workshops.

Key Features:

  • Speech & debate competitions
  • National & cultural day celebrations
  • Quiz bowls & intellectual contests
  • Moral guidance & leadership sessions
Safety & SecurityAREA 06

Safe, Secure & Monitored Campus

Comprehensive physical safety measures with controlled campus access, designated gates, and responsible staff supervision at all times.

Key Features:

  • Single-point secure visitor access
  • Staff monitoring during arrivals and dismissals
  • Clean hygiene standards & purified water
  • Emergency protocols and first-aid provisions
Physical Well-BeingAREA 07

Sports & Physical Recreation

Designated play areas and scheduled physical activities promoting teamwork, fitness, healthy competition, and motor skills.

Key Features:

  • Supervised outdoor play & exercise
  • Team sports fostering camaraderie
  • Annual sports days and athletic challenges
  • Emphasis on physical agility and mental vitality
Guidance & TutoringAREA 08

Dedicated Academic Support Centers

Specific after-hours study support and one-on-one concept clinics at Proud Academy for students requiring focused revision.

Key Features:

  • Specialized concept clearing sessions
  • Exam technique and past paper analysis
  • Frequent mock tests with actionable feedback
  • Small group remedial tutoring
Safety & Well-Being

Our Campus Safety & Discipline Protocol

Parents entrust us with their children's growth and well-being. We maintain diligent oversight, clear attendance logs, prompt gate dismissals, and attentive staff supervision throughout the operational day.

  • Strict visitor screening and designated single-entry access gates
  • Dedicated staff members assisting arrival and dismissal times
  • Clean drinking water filtration and hygienic sanitation amenities
  • Calm, quiet study zones free from unauthorized interruptions
